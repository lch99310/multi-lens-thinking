## How to use this template

1. Fill in every section you can. Sections you skip should be left in with `(not applicable)` so the lens knows it's not signal-missing.
2. Re-read every 6 months. The "current base", "career vision", and "blind spots" sections drift fastest.
3. Don't aspire — describe. The lens calibrates on who you are now; aspiration belongs in `memory.md` under "Active themes".
4. Be willing to write things you'd be uncomfortable showing publicly. This file is for AI collaboration, not for your LinkedIn.
